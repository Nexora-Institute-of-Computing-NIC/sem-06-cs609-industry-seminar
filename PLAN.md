# CS609 — Industry Seminar — PLAN

**Semester:** 6 · **Credits:** 1 · **Level:** Advanced
**Theory:** 15 hrs · **Seminar:** 15 hrs · **Tools:** Guest talks, panel discussions, industry case studies

## Course Format
A speaker series plus structured reflection. Invited guests from industry, research, and startups give talks on current topics. Students attend, ask questions, and write reflective summaries.

## Seminar Topics (guest series)

### Theme 1 — Industry and Career (4 talks)
1. How software teams actually ship — from startup to big tech
2. Engineering career paths — IC, manager, specialist
3. Interviewing at scale — what companies really test
4. Navigating your first year on the job

### Theme 2 — Current Technology (4 talks)
5. AI in production — real systems and real failures
6. Modern data platforms — what companies actually build
7. Security in practice — the attacker's perspective
8. Cloud at scale — cost, reliability, and the trade-offs

### Theme 3 — Research and Innovation (3 talks)
9. From research paper to shipped product
10. Open-source as a career
11. Deep tech vs applied — where to focus

### Theme 4 — Soft Skills and Growth (4 talks)
12. Technical writing that gets read
13. Cross-functional collaboration — working with design and product
14. Effective code review and mentoring
15. Negotiating your offer — salary, equity, role

## Practical Labs (15 — one per talk)

Each week:
1. **Pre-read** — skim the speaker's background and topic
2. **Attend** — 45-minute talk + 15-minute Q&A
3. **Ask one real question** during Q&A (or submit in writing)
4. **Write a 300-word reflection** in `reflections/week-NN.md` answering:
   - What was the most surprising insight?
   - What did you disagree with — and why?
   - One concrete thing you will do differently because of this talk

## Assessment

| Component | Weight |
| :--- | :---: |

## Guest-Request Guidelines

- Speakers should have 2+ years of experience in their topic.
- Mix of company sizes: startup, scale-up, large tech, research lab.
- Topics should be concrete — real systems, real numbers, real mistakes.

## If a Guest Is Unavailable

Replace the talk with:
- A recorded industry talk (e.g. from conferences) and the same reflection exercise
- A panel discussion with 2–3 local practitioners (remote is fine)
- A structured case study discussion led by the instructor
